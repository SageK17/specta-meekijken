# Specta bold site: 12 built-in marketing ideas + 4 homepage directions

16 Nov 2026. Built from study-jvo-design.md, study-jvo-social.md and study-specta-assets.md. Image paths are relative to `~/Specta/`.
Hard limits for every direction: €60 only. Approved wording only ("push down on the side ledge and up to seven cards rise in a stepped stack", genuine leather with a Saffiano finish, RFID-blocking that helps block wireless skimming, Black/Grey/Navy/Brown, free shipping included, gift box included, EU only, 30-day returns). No dimensions, "12 cards", "100%" or mechanism words. No reviews, stars, countdowns, stock lines or "free gift box". No v3b render files. AI images are shown as styled images at no more than 2x their native width (most are 1127x1400, so up to about 563 px CSS; studio photos up to 550 px; cutouts up to about 330 px; cat-hero-desk-black up to 700 px). They are never presented as customer photos.

## Part 1: the 12 strongest marketing ideas (and where each lives)

| # | Idea | What it does | Lives in |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **The tape strip** "€60 · FREE SHIPPING INCLUDED · 30-DAY RETURNS" | A fixed signature, like JVO's "ZONDAG 12:30" strip. It puts the all-in price and the risk remover next to every buy moment. | The bottom of the hero, under every buy button, the footer and the end card of every social post |
| M2 | **Move-first hero** "PUSH DOWN. / CARDS UP." with the accent full stop popping last | The distinctive asset and the peak moment. It uses a CSS clip-wipe with a 70 ms stagger and no video. | The hero on home and the first frame of the PDP gallery |
| M3 | **WANTED: YOUR CARD** (the gezocht poster) | Shows the problem first: "Last seen: bottom of your wallet. Reward: one press." Its tear-off strips copy the real product link (with UTM) as an honest share mechanic. | The problem band on home (this replaces the N2 wireframe cards) |
| M4 | **The reveal pair** (raad het): closed, then cards up | Solves the problem in one swap. A sticky crossfade between two studio stills carries the approved sentence as its caption. | Directly after M3 on home; the PDP how-it-works |
| M5 | **THE MATHS** (receipt or calculator showing 60.00) | Cost transparency: Specta 60.00 · shipping included · gift box included · TOTAL €60.00. It is the gain frame, never "was" or per-day. | A price section on home and the PDP buy box ("boarding-pass" ticket card) |
| M6 | **READ BEFORE USE** (the bijsluiter leaflet) | An honest facts panel in six numbered parts, including "Not for you if you carry coins or a stack of cards". Authority comes from verifiable facts. | The PDP facts panel and a teaser link on home |
| M7 | **RECIPE FOR A GOOD GIFT** (the recept card) with a "READY TO GIVE" stamp | The second audience, the Q4 gift buyer: serves 1 · difficulty: easy · gift box included. An order-by date is added only once the carrier route is real. | The warm gift band on home and the gift block on the PDP |
| M8 | **Colour as a choice game** ("Which one is yours? A Black B Grey C Navy D Brown") | A small first commitment without pressure. Each answer deep-links to `?variant=`, and Black stays preselected on the PDP. | The colour section on home (tiles, signpost or score-bug pills, depending on the direction) |
| M9 | **Fact stickers and stamps** (GIFT BOX INCLUDED, FREE SHIPPING INCLUDED, 30-DAY RETURNS, EU ONLY, PUSH DOWN) | Bold decoration where every sticker is a true fact. Never BESTSELLER, LIMITED or SALE. | Scattered over the hero, the gift band and the buy band, plus the matching Instagram story stickers |
| M10 | **Fact ticker / hairline spec strip** | The facts set as broadcast "instrumentation" (40 s linear ticker, edge masks), so plain facts look engineered without inventing specs. | Between the hero and the next section, and over the diagonal seam |
| M11 | **ASK SPECTA** (real questions only) | Removes objections: cash? how many cards? what RFID-blocking does and does not do? delivery? returns? why no reviews yet? | Above the final buy band on home and on the PDP |

The trust layer goes on every direction's buy band: payment icons including iDEAL, 30-day returns, the reply time and a contact link within one screen of the button. Only the buy button is a filled accent object.

## Part 2: four homepage directions

### A. CONTROL ROOM: "Tonight's broadcast: your cards"
- **Mood:** JVO open-day dusk broadcast. The site as a live production desk at night: score bugs, a ticker, `+` registration marks, a vertical side line, a `01/08` section counter and a cold ground with one warm key light. It feels precise, nocturnal and engineered.
- **Palette:** ground `#0B1120` · ground-2 `#141D33` · leather navy `#2D3A67` · accent cognac `#C9773F` (ink text only) · accent-2 `#E9A46A` (labels, gradient end) · ice `#F1EEE8` · ice-muted `rgba(241,238,232,.68)` · line `rgba(241,238,232,.12)`.
- **Fonts:** Saira Extra Condensed 800/900 (display, uppercase, line-height .84) + JetBrains Mono 500 (labels, .2em tracking) + Inter Tight 400 (body). This is deliberately not Big Shoulders, so Specta stays separate from JVO.
- **Hero concept:** an asymmetric poster split. On the left, mono eyebrow `LIVE FROM YOUR POCKET`, then "PUSH DOWN. / CARDS UP." (line 2 in the accent gradient), then the €60 score-bug number and the Buy button pinned to the bottom with the one offset double shadow. On the right, the product in a thin accent "lens" ring that sits half off-screen, on a radial key light (alpha .28), with a contact shadow and a 7 s float. A diagonal 9° band carries the M10 ticker across the seam. M1 sits at the bottom edge.
- **Section order:** 1 Hero → 2 Fact ticker → 3 "INCOMING: SEARCH" search-bar interlude (M12) → 4 WANTED: YOUR CARD, styled as a "missing person" broadcast lower-third (M3) → 5 Instant replay: closed → cards up, with frame counter `REPLAY 1/2` (M4) → 6 Line-up: four score-bug colour pills with 5px end bars in the leather colour, each showing its tile (M8) → 7 Scoreboard: split-flap "€60" over the M5 receipt rows → 8 Gift band "TONIGHT'S SPECIAL GUEST: THE GIFT BOX" on warm glow (M7) → 9 ASK SPECTA as a call-in panel (M11) → 10 Buy band + M1 + trust layer.
- **Images:** hero phone `_theme-v21/assets/cat-hero-phone-black.jpg`; hero desktop `design-system/assets/Product/cutout-black.png` in the ring (≤330 px) with `_theme-v21/assets/cat-pdp-black-1.jpg` as an inset "camera 2" monitor; replay `_theme-v21/assets/studio-navy-closed.jpg` + `_theme-v21/assets/studio-navy-front-cards-up.jpg`; line-up `_theme-v21/assets/cat-tile-{black,grey,navy,brown}.jpg`; gift `_theme-v21/assets/studio-black-gift-box.jpg` + `_theme-v21/assets/cat-gift-1.jpg`; detail monitor `_theme-v21/assets/studio-black-side.jpg`.
- **Uses:** M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M7, M8, M10, M11, M12. (M9 stickers become small status pills.)
- **Never:** LIVE dots used as urgency, countdowns, a clock, or "viewers now".

### B. POSTER CAMPAIGN: "Every scroll a new poster"
- **Mood:** a street-poster zine on cream paper. Each section is one full-viewport poster in a borrowed genre (wanted notice, recipe card, leaflet, signpost, film poster), with tape, grain, halftone and torn edges. It is loud and funny, but every word is true. One object per poster, and the condensed type does the scale work.
- **Palette:** paper `#EEE6D6` · paper-2 `#E2D5BD` · print white `#FBF8F1` · ink navy `#1F2747` · leather navy `#2D3A67` · accent brick `#B8572E` (ink text on it; white text only at ≥24px bold, to be checked) · tape `#F2C27E` · stamp red `#A8322A` (lines and stamps only).
- **Fonts:** Anton (display, uppercase) + IBM Plex Mono 500 (fact labels, credit blocks) + IBM Plex Sans 400 (body).
- **Hero concept:** a film poster. "SPECTA PRESENTS", then the cast line "BLACK · GREY · NAVY · BROWN", then the huge title "PUSH DOWN. CARDS UP." with the product cutout standing in the middle like the lead actor. The billing block at the bottom reads "starring your cards · costumes: genuine leather, Saffiano finish · sets: your front pocket · tickets €60, shipping included". It is taped to the page with a slight -1.5° rotation, and the M1 tape strip runs across the bottom corner. No premiere dates.
- **Section order (one poster each):** 1 Film poster hero (M2) → 2 WANTED: YOUR CARD with live tear-off strips (M3) → 3 "BEFORE / AFTER" split print, closed vs cards up (M4) → 4 Signpost "BLACK → GREY → NAVY → BROWN → AS A GIFT →" plus one pale board "THE OLD WALLET · ALWAYS AN OPTION" (M8) → 5 Receipt poster "THE MATHS" (M5) → 6 RECIPE FOR A GOOD GIFT with a "READY TO GIVE" stamp (M7) → 7 Leaflet teaser "READ BEFORE USE", parts 1–6 folded (M6) → 8 Poster wall strip of the AI campaign posters, each shown whole (M12) → 9 "ASK SPECTA" as a pinned Q&A sheet (M11) → 10 Buy poster "€60. THAT'S THE WHOLE PRICE." + stickers (M9) + trust layer.
- **Images:** hero `design-system/assets/Product/cutout-navy.png`; wanted `_theme-v21/assets/studio-black-closed.jpg` as the taped "last seen" print; before/after `_theme-v21/assets/studio-brown-closed.jpg` + `_theme-v21/assets/studio-brown-front-cards-up.jpg`; signpost thumbs `_theme-v21/assets/studio-{black,grey,navy,brown}-front-cards-up.jpg`; recipe `_theme-v21/assets/studio-brown-gift-box.jpg` as a polaroid; leaflet `design-system/assets/Product/mechanism-navy-cards-up.png`; wall `_theme-v21/assets/cat-wall-5.jpg`, `cat-wall-7.jpg`, `cat-wall-8.jpg`, `cat-wall-1.jpg` (same folder).
- **Uses:** M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8, M9, M11, M12.
- **Never:** "LUKT ALTIJD"/"always works", frequency-sorted side effects, invented chat screenshots, or a weather "100%" gag.

### C. RETAIL EDITORIAL: "The drop" (Pull&Bear / H&M full-bleed)
- **Mood:** a fashion retailer's campaign landing. Edge-to-edge colour fields, very large tight sans type set over the image, a thin header that turns solid once scrolling starts (fixes N1), stacked full-width tiles with one label and an arrow each, and a shoppable hotspot showing the real price. It is confident, clean, loud by scale and quiet by detail.
- **Palette:** off-white `#F4F1EC` · black `#111111` · leather black `#29282E` · terracotta field `#B9643A` (from the cat-hero-desk series; black text on it) · leather navy `#2D3A67` · leather brown `#B46E40` · grey field `#8E8D91` · warm dark gift `#1E1611`.
- **Fonts:** Archivo 800 at width 125 (expanded display, uppercase, tight tracking -.02em) + Inter Tight 400/600 (UI, labels, body).
- **Hero concept:** one screen and one image. On desktop, a full-bleed terracotta field with `cat-hero-desk-black` placed at ≤700 px and the field extending its background, with "CARDS UP." in 18vw expanded type running behind and past the wallet. Bottom-left: "Push down on the side ledge and up to seven cards rise in a stepped stack." with SHOP BLACK — €60. A hotspot on the wallet reads "Specta · €60 · free shipping included". On phone, the black portrait hero.
- **Section order:** 1 Full-bleed hero (M2) → 2 Four stacked full-width colour tiles, each a leather-coloured field with its tile photo and one label "NAVY →" (M8) → 3 "STILL DIGGING?" bold graphic problem (a thick-stroke flat stack) next to the cards-up photo (M3 light, M4) → 4 Lookbook band, three lifestyle images in an asymmetric editorial grid with small captions "Front pocket.", "Every card. One press." (no customer framing) → 5 Billboard band `cat-wall-1` with the search-bar interlude overlaid (M12) → 6 Warm dark gift band "READY TO GIVE." with real box + mood shot (M7) → 7 Hairline spec strip + "THE MATHS" line (M10, M5) → 8 ASK SPECTA accordion (M11) → 9 Buy band + M1 + trust layer.
- **Images:** hero desktop `_theme-v21/assets/cat-hero-desk-black.jpg` (alternates `cat-hero-desk-brown.jpg`, `cat-hero-desk-grey.jpg`); hero phone `_theme-v21/assets/cat-hero-phone-black.jpg`; tiles `_theme-v21/assets/cat-tile-{black,grey,navy,brown}.jpg`; problem/after `_theme-v21/assets/studio-navy-front-cards-up.jpg`; lookbook `_theme-v21/assets/cat-life-1.jpg`, `cat-life-3.jpg`, `cat-life-5.jpg`, plus `cat-about-3.jpg` (car boot, golden hour); billboard `_theme-v21/assets/cat-wall-1.jpg`; gift `_theme-v21/assets/cat-gift-1.jpg` + `_theme-v21/assets/studio-black-gift-box.jpg`; spec `_theme-v21/assets/hp-saffiano-four-colours.jpg`.
- **Uses:** M1, M2, M4, M5, M7, M8, M10, M11, M12 (M3 as a short line only).
- **Never:** stretched photos, so full-bleed always means colour field plus product; no SALE/NEW-IN badges and no "only X left".

### D. HYPERREAL WORLD: "Specta in the wild" (JVO 3D-scene approach)
- **Mood:** JVO's hyperreal Blender trick, where the message lives inside a believable object, with the approved AI catalog renders as the set pieces (no Blender wallet until the phone scan exists). Each section is a "scene" you walk into: a night street, a calculator on a desk, a departure board, an amber-lit gift table. It is cinematic and dark, with deep reflections and contact shadows, and type that sits in the scene like signage.
- **Palette:** night `#07090F` · asphalt `#151820` · deep navy `#1B2440` · leather navy `#2D3A67` · amber key `#E39A4E` (ink text on it) · sodium `#F4C77D` · signage white `#F5F3EE` · neon red `#D9473A` (campaign art only, lines only).
- **Fonts:** Syne 800 (display, uppercase, wide and cinematic) + Space Mono 400/700 (signage, departure board, receipts) + DM Sans 400 (body).
- **Hero concept:** a surreal set piece, `crazy-navy-splash` (the navy wallet rising out of a navy splash) at ≤563 px, centred on a full-bleed deep-navy field that extends its tones with a radial glow and grain. A split-flap departure-board line flips in "CARDS UP — NOW BOARDING" letter by letter (40–60 ms), with "PUSH DOWN." above it and the €60 Buy button below. M1 is styled as the board's bottom row.
- **Section order:** 1 Splash hero (M2) → 2 "Scene 01: the street", the `cat-wall-1` billboard as an establishing shot with the search bar typing on a phone screen (M12; no "seen in stations" caption) → 3 "Scene 02: the bottom of the wallet", WANTED notice pinned to a dark pinboard (M3) → 4 "Scene 03: the reveal", macro dark close-up `cat-pdp-black-1` with the approved sentence as a subtitle (M4) → 5 "Scene 04: the desk", a CSS calculator or receipt reading 60.00 (M5) → 6 "Scene 05: the line-up", all four in the car boot at golden hour, linking to colours (M8) → 7 "Scene 06: the gift table", amber spotlight + the real box + recipe card (M7) → 8 "Scene 07: the noir poster" `cat-wall-3` "SHARP." → 9 ASK SPECTA as a lit info kiosk (M11) → 10 Buy band "FINAL BOARDING: €60" + M1 + trust layer.
- **Images:** hero `launch-kit-backup/library/crazy-navy-splash.jpg` (C0166; export it into `_theme-v21/assets` first; fallback `_theme-v21/assets/cat-wall-2.jpg` "Meet SPECTA.", whose baked text the H1 must not repeat); street `_theme-v21/assets/cat-wall-1.jpg`; pinboard print `_theme-v21/assets/studio-black-closed.jpg`; reveal `_theme-v21/assets/cat-pdp-black-1.jpg`; line-up `_theme-v21/assets/cat-about-3.jpg`; gift `_theme-v21/assets/cat-gift-1.jpg` + `_theme-v21/assets/studio-black-gift-box.jpg` + `_theme-v21/assets/cat-wall-4.jpg` ("Carry first class"); noir `_theme-v21/assets/cat-wall-3.jpg`; person interlude `_theme-v21/assets/cat-life-1.jpg`.
- **Uses:** M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M7, M8, M11, M12 (M10 as departure-board rows).
- **Never:** a 3D-modelled wallet, mock placements captioned as real, the retired coral outside the noir poster, or departure times/dates that look like deadlines.

## How the four differ at a glance
| | A Control room | B Poster campaign | C Retail editorial | D Hyperreal world |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Night ink + key light | Cream paper + grain | Off-white + full colour fields | Near-black cinematic scenes |
| Loud thing | Score-bug numbers, ticker | Genre posters, stamps | Giant expanded type over the image | Set pieces + split-flap board |
| Photos | Monitors / insets | Taped polaroids | Full-bleed fields, lookbook | Scenes with deep reflections |
| Problem moment | Broadcast lower-third | Wanted poster | One bold graphic line | Pinboard in a dark room |
