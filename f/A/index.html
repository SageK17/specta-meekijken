Line 1Does cash fit?

A few folded notes, yes. Coins, no. If you carry a coin purse's worth of change, Specta isn't for you.

Line 2How many cards?

Up to seven, depending on how thick your cards are. Push down on the side ledge and they rise in a stepped stack, so you see every edge.

Line 3What does RFID-blocking do?

The lining helps block wireless skimming of the cards inside the wallet. It's an extra layer of protection, not a guarantee, and it can't protect a card once it's out of the wallet.

Within the EU only. Shipping is free and already included in the €60, so nothing is added at checkout.
