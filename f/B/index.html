How many cards fit?

Up to seven, depending on how thick they are.

Can I carry cash or coins?

Specta is made for cards. If you carry coins, it is not the wallet for you.

What does RFID-blocking do?

The lining helps block wireless skimming of the cards inside. It is one layer of protection, not a guarantee against every kind of fraud.

What does delivery cost?

Nothing extra. Free shipping is included in the €60. We ship within the EU only.
