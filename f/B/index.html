Questions?

Questions you might have. Straight answers.

How many cards fit?

Up to seven, depending on how thick they are.

Can I carry cash or coins?

Specta is made for cards. If you carry coins, it is not the wallet for you.

What does RFID-blocking do?

The lining helps block wireless skimming of the cards inside. It is one layer of protection, not a guarantee against every kind of fraud.

What does delivery cost, and how long does it take?

Nothing extra: shipping is included in the €60. We ship to all 27 EU countries; delivery takes 6–10 working days.

What if it's not for me?

You have 30 days to return it. Return postage is paid by you. The full terms are in our returns policy.

Is it a good gift?

It ships in its own gift box, included in the €60. If the color isn't right, the 30-day return window applies.
