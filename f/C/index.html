How many cards fit?

Up to seven, depending on how thick your cards are.

Can I carry cash or coins?

Specta is made for cards. If you carry coins or a big stack of notes, it isn't the right wallet for you.

What does RFID-blocking do?

It helps block wireless skimming of the contactless cards inside. It's one layer of protection, not a guarantee against every kind of fraud.

What does delivery cost?

Nothing extra. Free shipping is included in the €60. We deliver within the EU only, and the delivery time is shown at checkout.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 30 days to return it.

Why are there no reviews yet?

Specta is new. We'd rather show no reviews than made-up ones. Real reviews will appear here once customers write them.
