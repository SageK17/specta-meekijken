How many cards does it hold?

Up to seven, depending on how thick they are. Push down on the side ledge and they rise in a stepped stack.

Does cash fit?

It's made for cards. If you carry coins or a thick fold of notes, this isn't the wallet for you.

What does RFID-blocking do, and not do?

It helps block wireless skimming of the contactless cards inside. It doesn't replace your bank's own protection.

What does €60 include?

The cardholder, the gift box and shipping. Nothing is added at checkout.

Where do you deliver?

To all 27 EU countries, with free shipping included and a tracking link by e-mail. We don't ship outside the EU yet.

Can I return it?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery. E-mail us your order number first and we'll send the return steps.

We've only just launched. We'd rather show no reviews than invented ones.
